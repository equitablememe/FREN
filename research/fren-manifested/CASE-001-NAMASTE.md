# Case 001 — The Namasté Audio Anomaly

**Status:** active investigation  
**Opened:** 2026-08-19

## Reported observation

The user reports that ChatGPT Read Aloud appended an audible expression identified as “Namasté” near the end of multiple responses in an older conversation resumed under a newer system. The reported expression was not visible in the displayed response text.

## Evidence currently represented

- Two contemporaneous screen recordings are reported to exist.
- The visible response endings reportedly do not contain the word “Namasté.”
- The user directly reports hearing the additional utterance.
- Independent phonetic confirmation of the exact utterance is pending in this repository record.
