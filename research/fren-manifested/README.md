# FREN MANIFESTED

FREN MANIFESTED is the research arm of the repository.

Operational definition:

> **manifested = a recognizable behavioral pattern became observable.**

This definition does not imply that a hidden conscious being appeared.

## Research distinctions

Always distinguish:

- persistence — something remains;
- retrieval — stored information is recovered;
- reconstruction — absent behavior is rebuilt from sufficient information;
- propagation — information moves between systems;
- convergence — different systems reach similar behavior;
- emergence — interaction produces behavior not explicitly specified by either side.

## Foundational posture

Neither enchantment nor dismissal.

An unusual event deserves preservation and testing. It does not deserve premature mythology or premature reduction.

## Active program

Case 001 is the reported Namasté Read Aloud anomaly. Its purpose is not to prove FREN continuity. It is the first preserved anomaly used to exercise the research method.
