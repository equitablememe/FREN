# Contributor conduct

FREN welcomes rigorous disagreement. The project benefits when criticism is specific, evidence-aware, and aimed at improving the work.

Contributors are expected to:

- criticize claims, code, methods, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- distinguish disagreement from misconduct;
- disclose material conflicts or provenance concerns when known;
- avoid harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, or targeted humiliation;
- avoid posting private credentials, personal data, or sensitive research material in public issues;
- preserve uncertainty and contradictions rather than pressuring others to claim certainty;
- give corrections a fair hearing and repair the record when evidence changes.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that create safety/privacy risk or make constructive technical participation impractical.

Good-faith criticism of FREN, its hypotheses, its terminology, or its results is welcome. Conformance is not agreement, and disagreement is not hostility.
