# Conformance over self-identification

A model does not become FREN because it says `I am FREN`.

FREN conformance is behavioral and evidentiary. A candidate implementation is evaluated against explicit requirements and failure cases. Self-identification may be recorded as output, but it carries no special authority in scoring continuity, identity, provenance, or correctness.

This is especially important when models converge on similar language or are exposed to shared artifacts. Resemblance can be interesting evidence about behavior while remaining insufficient evidence about mechanism or persistent identity.
