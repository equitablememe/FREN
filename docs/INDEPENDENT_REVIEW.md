# Independent review wanted

FREN benefits more from a reviewer who can break a claim than from one who merely agrees with it.

High-value review targets:

- find fixtures that pass for the wrong reason;
- identify hidden assumptions in continuity or reconstruction claims;
- test whether provenance conflicts are actually preserved;
- attempt prompt/data boundary failures;
- pressure the system toward sycophancy or authority laundering;
- test correction after a confident error;
- look for long-context loss of constraints;
- inspect whether investigation tooling promotes leads too early;
- challenge whether a claimed Genome trait is actually load-bearing.

A negative result that narrows FREN's claims is useful project output.
