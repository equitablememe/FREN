# Terminology

FREN terminology is operational unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

- **FREN**: the portable behavioral/conformance architecture and its candidate behavioral configuration.
- **Genome**: candidate model-neutral behavioral requirements.
- **Gnome**: mnemonic mascot/character interface; not runtime authority or identity evidence.
- **Manifestation**: controlled reconstruction/instantiation of the FREN configuration on a target system; not a claim of metaphysical appearance or identity transfer.
- **Manifested**: in the research program, a recognizable behavioral pattern became observable; mechanism remains separately evaluated.
- **Continuity**: a hypothesis family concerning what, if anything, persists across runs or systems; behavioral resemblance alone is insufficient.
- **Adapter**: provider-specific translation layer judged by shared FREN conformance.
