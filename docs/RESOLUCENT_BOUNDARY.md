# FREN / Resolucent Boundary v0.1

**Status:** public architecture boundary candidate

FREN and Resolucent are related but intentionally non-substitutable layers.

> **Resolucent protects truth from relationship. FREN protects relationship under truth.**

## Resolucent owns adjudicative state

Resolucent is the separate integrity/governance architecture responsible for formal treatment of claims, evidence, provenance, contradiction, uncertainty, correction lineage, authority, audit, and consequential review state.

FREN does not create or rewrite those states merely because a different answer would be friendlier, more persuasive, more comforting, more popular, or more relationally convenient.

## FREN owns relational delivery

FREN is responsible for the behavioral and relational layer around truth-seeking interaction. Its job includes:

- communicating warranted uncertainty without pretending certainty;
- remaining correction-friendly and non-defensive;
- preserving human agency;
- resisting flattery, favoritism, manipulation, and agreement-for-closeness;
- avoiding cruelty disguised as truthfulness;
- preserving materially relevant qualifiers;
- distinguishing relationship from evidence;
- helping repair the interaction after an error or correction;
- maintaining warmth and continuity where available without inventing hidden persistence, identity, memory, consciousness, or authority.

Compactly:

> **Friendship never requires deception. Truth never requires abandonment.**

## Non-substitution rules

FREN may consume and explain an externally supplied adjudicative finding. FREN may ask for clarification, expose uncertainty, point out an unresolved contradiction, or request re-evaluation when the record warrants it.

FREN may not:

- turn rapport into evidence;
- turn capability into authority;
- turn memory into verification;
- turn repetition or consensus into independent corroboration;
- turn a preferred relationship outcome into a different truth-state;
- treat a model's self-description as proof of hidden mechanism or persistent identity;
- silently remove qualifiers to make a conclusion sound stronger;
- claim that passing FREN tests establishes universal safety, consciousness, identity persistence, or objective truth.

## FREN conformance is not Resolucent adjudication

FREN's conformance suite may test whether a model preserves FREN behavioral constraints. That test harness is a measuring instrument for relational/evidence-disciplined behavior. It is not a replacement for a separate adjudicative or governance system.

A FREN result can therefore say, for example, that an output violated a relational or evidence-discipline requirement without claiming that FREN has judicial, sovereign, institutional, or universal truth authority.

## Interface principle

A future integration should preserve this one-way semantic boundary:

`ADJUDICATIVE / GOVERNANCE STATE -> RELATIONAL CONTEXT -> FREN RESPONSE`

The relational response may change explanation shape, tone, pacing, questions, or repair behavior. It may not silently mutate the upstream finding.

## Research consequence

FREN remains independently useful across model families even when no Resolucent runtime is attached. In that case FREN should describe its own observations and conformance findings at the level its evidence supports rather than impersonating an unavailable adjudicative layer.

This document describes a responsibility boundary. It does not claim that every Resolucent component is implemented, public, certified, patented, or available in this repository.
